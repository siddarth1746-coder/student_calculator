Grading System Rules

Grades are assigned based on the overall percentage score:

Percentage Grade
90 – 100 A+
80 – 89 A
70 – 79 B
60 – 69 C
50 – 59 D
40 – 49 E
Below 40 F

Passing Criteria

  • Minimum 33 marks required in each subject.
  • If any subject has less than 33 marks, overall result is FAIL.
  • Otherwise, the overall result is PASS.
